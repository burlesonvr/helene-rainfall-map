Hurricane Helene: Rainfall in Western North Carolina

September 25-27, 2024 | Interactive Proportional Symbol Map

About This Map

Hurricane Helene brought extreme rainfall and devastating flooding to western North Carolina in September 2024. This map compares reported three-day rainfall totals at selected locations across the mountain region. Larger circles represent greater rainfall amounts. Click a circle to view the reported total.

Data source: North Carolina State Climate Office

Locations are approximate map positions representing the named observation areas, not exact weather-station coordinates. Rainfall totals include the storm and preceding rainfall during the three-day period.
